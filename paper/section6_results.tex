% ==================================================================
%
% Numerical values in Tables III, V (severity), and X (economics) are

% (artifacts/checkpoints_am_mappo_v2/am_mappo_final.pt) under the

This section reports quantitative results from the AM-focused tri-layer
framework. The slow-timescale market clearing dataset is generated in
\texttt{data/precomputed\_365d\_97to67} (365 days, 15-minute resolution,
35\,040 samples; 345 days for training, 20 days held out for evaluation).
The fast-timescale FFR and topology-generalisation tables are produced by
\texttt{src/eval/eval\_ffr\_topology.py} (six methods, four scenarios, 20 runs
per cell). Economic results are produced by \texttt{src/eval/eval\_economics.py}.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Architecture note.} All three layers (L0 DSO pricing,
L1 VPP dispatch, L2 GraphSAGE-MAPPO control) are oriented around the AM
service portfolio (energy P + FFR capacity/activation). A reactive (Q)
product has been intentionally excluded to keep the optimisation
landscape and revenue accounting consistent across layers; reactive
support, when needed, is provided implicitly via inverter Volt/Var
control rather than as a remunerated market product.

per scenario and per method, averaged over 20 runs across the 15 training

exposes the controllers to a substantially wider distribution of
switching states than a single-topology evaluation, which is why all
methods (including the proposed) miss the 49.5\,Hz UFLS bound on a
non-trivial fraction of seeds. Even so, the proposed GraphSAGE-MAPPO

\subsubsection{Cooperative Primary--Secondary--FFR Dispatch}
\label{ssec:cooperative_dispatch}

Fig.~\ref{fig:cooperative_dispatch} decomposes the total power
injection during each scenario into its three sources for the proposed
controller: VPP-dispatched FFR ($p_{\text{FFR},\text{total}}$, the
filtered output of T$_{\text{BESS}}$=100~ms, T$_{\text{V2G}}$=300~ms,
T$_{\text{PV-curtail}}$=1~s), GFM primary droop ($p_{\text{gov}}$, with
$T_g$=1~s and $1/R_{\text{SYS}}\approx21$~pu), and the GFM secondary
AGC ($p_{\text{ref},\text{AGC}}$, armed after a 10~s delay).
The three traces visually confirm the cooperative dispatch design
introduced in Subsection~\ref{ssec:multitimescale_flow}: FFR peaks
within 0--2~s after the event, GFM primary droop fills in the 1--10~s
window as the FFR filter response saturates, and the AGC takes over
after $\sim$10~s to drive $\Delta f$ back to nominal. The integral
freeze imposed during $\mathbf{1}_{\text{FFR active}}=1$
(cf.~\cite{subotic2023dualportgfm}) prevents the AGC overshoot that
would otherwise occur when FFR fades because of SoC limits, and the
priority ordering matches the Nordic FCR$_{\text{I}}$ recommendation
that converter-interfaced storage discharge precede slower
turbogenerator-equivalent reserves~\cite{mota2024fcri,papakonstantinou2021bessagc}.

\begin{figure}[!t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.98\columnwidth]{figures/fig_cooperative_dispatch.png}
\caption{Cooperative dispatch decomposition for the four scenarios.
$p_{\text{FFR},\text{total}}$ (blue): VPP fast frequency reserve;
$p_{\text{gov}}$ (orange): GFM primary droop, $T_g$=1~s;
$p_{\text{ref},\text{AGC}}$ (green): secondary AGC, $10$~s delay.}

\subsection{Topology Adaptation}
\label{ssec:topo}
% =====================================================================

Table~\ref{tab:topo} compares the FFR success rate on the 11 training
versus 5 unseen test topologies (farthest-point split of the 16-topology
cache), using S2 as the canonical stress scenario. On the train split
the proposed method already doubles the FFR-SR of the non-learning
baselines (18.2\,\% vs.\ 9.1\,\%), and on the held-out unseen
topologies every controller drops to 0\,\% --- S2 is sufficiently
severe to violate the 49.5\,Hz UFLS bound on every unseen
reconfiguration in the present cache. The pattern that survives is the
IAE and nadir margin: even at zero pass rate, the proposed method
remains nearest to the threshold (nadir gap $-0.08$\,Hz) and lowest
in unseen-topology IAE, whereas Fixed Droop and No FFR cross the bound
by 0.18--0.19\,Hz. We attribute the remaining gap on unseen topologies
primarily to two factors: (i)~the topology cache currently contains
switch-state variations whose Jaccard edge distance is dominated by
zero-impedance shortcuts, leaving the underlying \texttt{net.line}
adjacency near-constant; (ii)~the trained baselines MLP-MAPPO,
GCNN-PPO, MATD3 are not yet integrated, so the present comparison
reflects only the learning-vs.\ rule axis. Both items are tracked as
revision-time follow-ups.

\subsection{Economic Evaluation}
\label{ssec:economic}
% =====================================================================

To complement the security analysis we evaluate the dual-market revenue and
profit each method generates under the prices that clear the EM and AM
markets. Prices follow the framework already encoded in the project's reward
function (see \texttt{src/layer2\_control/reward.py}):
\[
R = \underbrace{\lambda_e P}_{\text{EM payment}}
  + \underbrace{\lambda_{\text{cap}} R_{\text{commit}}
              + \lambda_{\text{act}} R_{\text{del}} \mathbf{1}_{\text{event}}}_{\text{AM payment}}
  - \underbrace{c_{\text{us}}\,\lambda_{\text{act}} (R_{\text{commit}}-R_{\text{del}})^{+}}_{\text{undersupply}}
  - \text{OPEX}.
\]
With $\lambda_e=50$~\euro/MWh,
$\lambda_{\text{cap}}=50$~\euro/MW/h, $\lambda_{\text{act}}=100$~\euro/MWh,
$c_{\text{us}}=3$ (Nordic), and OPEX coefficients (PV 12, Wind 30,
BESS 7.5)~\euro/kW/year from IRENA 2023.

\paragraph{Method-level economic comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:economic_methods} compares the three measured methods on a
system-wide daily basis. The proposed controller is the only one that
actively commits FFR capacity --- AM-cap revenue of \euro\,157/day is
17\,$\times$ Fixed Droop and 100\,$\times$ No FFR --- and produces the
highest gross revenue (\euro\,833/day vs.\ 725 and 636). All three
methods, however, are net-negative under the present price deck because
the configured asset OPEX (\euro\,2\,027/day at IRENA-2023 PV/Wind/BESS
rates for the modelled placement) exceeds gross AM + energy revenue.
The proposed method also incurs the largest undersupply penalty
(\euro\,1\,136/day) precisely because it is the only controller that
books substantial reserve capacity that cannot always be delivered
under the most severe events; lighter or more conservative commitment
strategies trade higher pass rates for smaller penalties at the cost of
abandoning the AM revenue stream entirely (e.g.\ No FFR books almost
nothing and incurs zero penalty but also realises zero AM revenue).
The economic separator on which the proposed method dominates is
therefore AM-side gross cashflow + activation revenue at fixed
infrastructure cost; turning that gross-revenue lead into net
profitability requires either a higher FFR capacity price or a less
punitive undersupply multiplier than the 3$\times$ Nordic default used
here, both of which are policy choices outside the controller scope.

\subsection{Summary}
\label{ssec:summary}
% =====================================================================

\textbf{Claim 1 (FFR effectiveness, Subsec.~\ref{ssec:results_am}).}
Across the four scenarios and 15-topology training split, the proposed
GraphSAGE-MAPPO achieves the lowest post-event IAE and highest nadir
margin against both non-learning baselines, with FFR-SR 1.5--5\,$\times$
the Fixed Droop and No FFR pass rates under the severe S2 and S4
contingencies. The result is conditional on the present cache of
topologies and trained baselines; full quantitative ranking against
MLP-MAPPO, GCNN-PPO and MATD3 awaits their training.

\textbf{Claim 2 (Topology adaptation, Subsec.~\ref{ssec:topo}).}
On held-out feeder reconfigurations (5 unseen topologies), the proposed
method preserves the smallest IAE growth and nadir gap relative to the
training distribution (Table~\ref{tab:topo}). The current cache exposes
limited edge-distance variability (the switch states differ but the
underlying \texttt{net.line} adjacency is near-constant), so the
inductive-generalisation slope cannot yet be measured cleanly --- the
strongest empirical signal of the GraphSAGE encoder advantage will be
in the planned MLP-MAPPO ablation.

Pending the MLP-MAPPO ablation training. The architecture comparison

\textbf{Claim 4 (Economic effectiveness, Subsec.~\ref{ssec:economic}).}
The proposed method generates the highest gross daily revenue
(\euro\,833 vs.\ 725 / 636) and 17\,$\times$ the AM capacity revenue of
Fixed Droop. Under the present asset OPEX deck none of the three
measured methods is strictly net-positive on a daily basis; the
proposed method buys higher security (FFR-SR up to 1.5--5\,$\times$
baselines on the severe events) at the cost of a larger undersupply
exposure. Net profitability is a function of policy choices on FFR
capacity price and undersupply multiplier that lie outside the
controller scope; the controller-level claim is the gross-revenue +
security improvement, not the absolute profit sign.
